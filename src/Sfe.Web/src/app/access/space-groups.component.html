A group is a set of rights: what its members may do in this space (browse, download, tag, scan, change settings…). Accounts get rights only through their groups.

Every space has three built-in groups:

  • Administrators manage the space: its accounts, groups, settings, tags and scans.
  • Users are for everyday use: browse, play, download and tag.
  • Guests holds what visitors without an account may do (only look).

Make your own groups for anything in between. For example, Kids may browse and play but not download: put the children's accounts in Kids only.
