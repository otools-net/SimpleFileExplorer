Groups {{ groups().length }}

A group is a set of rights: what its members may do in this space (browse, download, tag, scan, change settings...). Accounts get rights only through their groups.

Every space has three built-in groups:

  • Administrators — manage the space: its accounts, groups, settings, tags and scans.
  • Users — everyday use: browse, play, download, tag.
  • Guests — what visitors without an account may do (only looking).

Make your own for anything in between. Example: Kids, who may browse and play but not download; put the children's accounts in Kids only.

Visitors without an account

Anyone who reaches the server can browse a space open to visitors, without signing in: the sign-in page offers a button for each such space. They get the rights of the Guests group, and never a right that changes something.

Example: open a Holiday photos space so relatives on your network can look at it, while Family stays private.

For someone who should sign in but only look, make a guest account instead (New account › Guest account).
