Without rules, every group's rights apply in every folder. A rule narrows that for one folder and everything below it, for a group or one account:

  • Hidden: they don't see the folder at all; it answers as if it weren't there.
  • Read only: they see its files and use them with their rights (play, download, tag), but nothing that changes the files themselves (editing pictures, deleting duplicates).
  • Read & write: their rights apply in full.

The nearest rule above a folder decides: hide Private and open Private/Shared again, and Private only shows the way to Shared.

Groups add up: an account sees a folder when any of its groups does. To hide a folder from the kids, hide it from every group they are in. A rule for one account only adds to what its groups give.
