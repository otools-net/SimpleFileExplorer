An account is a person (or a device) that signs in with a user name and a password. What it sees depends on its spaces: it is in a space when it is in at least one of the space's groups, and those groups decide what it may do there.

Example: anna — Family: Administrators · Work: Users. Anna sees both spaces, manages Family and only uses Work.
