Kind of account

A regular account gets exactly the rights of its groups.

A guest account signs in like any other but can only look: it never gets a right that changes something (tagging, scanning, settings, deleting duplicates...), even from a group that grants it. Example: a shared living-room-tv account for the family's television.

Not to be confused with visitors without an account, who don't sign in at all: a space lets them in from its own page (Visitors), with the rights of its Guests group.

Spaces and groups

An account sees a space when it is in at least one of the space's groups. The groups decide what it may do there; in several groups, it gets the rights of all of them.

Example:

  • anna — Family: Administrators · Work: Users
  • tom — Family: Users, Kids

Anna manages the Family space and uses the Work space; Tom only sees Family, with the rights of Users and Kids together. Someone in several spaces switches between them from the ⋮ menu.
