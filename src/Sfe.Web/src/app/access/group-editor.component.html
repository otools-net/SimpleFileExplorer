What its members may do

Each box is a right: something the group's members may do in this space. Leave it unticked and they can't.

An account in several groups gets the rights of all of them together. Example: tom is in Users (browse, play, tag) and in Downloaders (download files): he may do all four.

A right greyed out can't be given here: guests may never change anything, and an administrator of the server may switch a right off for a whole space or for the whole server.

In each part, the rights that only look come first. Changes things marks a right that changes the space (its index, tags, settings or accounts); Changes files one that creates, changes or deletes files in the library's folders: give those with care.
