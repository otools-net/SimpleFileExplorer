Mountains, day three

We left the hut before sunrise. The path climbed through the last larches, then over loose stones, and the valley below slowly filled with light. By nine we were on the ridge, with the lake far down on one side and the glacier on the other.

Lunch at the pass: bread, cheese, the last of the apricots. A family of marmots watched us from a rock and whistled every time someone stood up. The wind turned cold after noon, so we did not wait for the clouds to lift from the summit.

The way down was longer than it looked from above. Our knees will remember it. Dinner in the village, then early to bed: tomorrow the train back, and three hundred photos to sort.
